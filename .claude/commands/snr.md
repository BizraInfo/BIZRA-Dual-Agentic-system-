# /snr - Signal-to-Noise Ratio Optimization

## Activation
When user invokes `/snr [content]` or needs content quality optimization.

## SNR Formula
```
SNR = Signal / Noise
```

## Signal Components

### Technical Markers (+points)
- `formal`, `invariant`, `proof` (+3 each)
- `verification`, `optimization` (+2 each)
- `ihsan`, `deterministic` (+2 each)

### Confidence Weighting
- High confidence claims (+2)
- Verified statements (+3)
- Cited sources (+2)

### Wisdom Alignment (+points)
- `quran`, `hadith`, `sunnah` (+2 each)
- `hikmah`, `ihsan`, `bayt` (+2 each)
- `sovereign`, `genesis` (+1 each)

### Interdisciplinary Density (+points)
- `thermodynamics`, `topology` (+2 each)
- `jurisprudence`, `economics` (+2 each)
- `biological`, `quantum` (+2 each)
- Cross-domain synthesis (+3)

### Repair Bonus
- Self-correction acknowledgment (+2)
- `<promise>fixed</promise>` pattern (+3)

### Shannon Entropy
- Information density per byte
- Novel information ratio

## Noise Components

### Fluff Phrases (-points)
- "as an ai" (-3)
- "i understand" (-2)
- "to be honest" (-2)
- "sorry" (-1)
- "in conclusion" (-2)
- "it's important to note" (-2)
- Hedge words: "maybe", "perhaps", "might" (-1 each)

### Repetition Penalty
- >15% single word frequency (-3)
- Redundant sentences (-2 each)
- Circular explanations (-3)

### Speculation Penalty
- Ungrounded claims (-2 each)
- "I believe" without evidence (-2)
- Assumptions without justification (-2)

- Optimal: SNR >= 2.0
- Acceptable: SNR >= 1.5
- Needs Improvement: SNR < 1.5
